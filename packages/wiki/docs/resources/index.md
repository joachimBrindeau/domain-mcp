# MCP resources

Read-only context resources available to connected MCP clients.

- [`Account Information`](/docs/resources/account-information) — Current Dynadot account info: balance, email, limits
- [`My Domains`](/docs/resources/my-domains) — All domains in your Dynadot account with expiry dates
- [`My Contacts`](/docs/resources/my-contacts) — All WHOIS contacts in your Dynadot account
- [`My Folders`](/docs/resources/my-folders) — All folders in your Dynadot account
